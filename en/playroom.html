Time to play

A playroom for little explorers

Carefully planned zones encourage movement, imagination and social play, while parents enjoy a comfortable café overlooking the play area.

One zone, one new adventure

Climbing structure

Slides, nets and climbing routes for active play.

Recommended age: 4–12

Ball pit

A soft, cheerful space for jumping, hiding and play.

Recommended age: 2–8

Mini trampolines

Controlled movement, little jumps and plenty of laughter.

Recommended age: 4–12

Baby corner

A separate, enclosed soft-play area for our youngest guests.

Recommended age: 0–3

Creative corner

Drawing, building blocks and quieter activities for little makers.

Recommended age: 3–10

Role-play corner

Mini kitchen, shop and doctor’s office for imaginative play.

Recommended age: 3–8
For parents too

Coffee, Wi‑Fi and a view of the playroom

The parent café sits beside the play area with a clear view towards the children. Coffee, soft drinks and free Wi‑Fi are available.
